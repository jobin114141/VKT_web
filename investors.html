Massive Market Opportunity

Logistics Sector

India's logistics industry is projected to reach ₹23 lakh crore by 2030, driven by e-commerce, manufacturing growth, and supply chain modernization.

Road freight dominates 70–85% of logistics movement. Organized players represent only a small portion, leaving enormous room for professionally managed operators like VKT.

Travel & Tourism Sector

Domestic travel continues to expand post-COVID through rising middle-class spending, group tourism, student excursions, and interstate travel growth.

With a captive college market, growing brand visibility, and operational expertise, VKT is positioned to scale this division significantly.
