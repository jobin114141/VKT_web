Our Story

What began in Aluva, Kerala, as a focused regional transport operation has evolved into a formidable national platform. Over the years, VKT Logistics LLP has systematically expanded its reach, navigating the complexities of the Indian landscape with a steady commitment to operational integrity.

From our first fleet of vehicles to today's integrated multi-division mobility ecosystem, our journey is defined by the trust of our clients and the dedication of our workforce. We didn't just grow in scale; we grew in technical capability, integrating sophisticated logistics technology to manage cargo, warehousing, and corporate mobility with surgical precision.

Today, we stand as a symbol of Indian industrial resilience, bridging the gap between demand and delivery across the subcontinent.

OUR HISTORY

Milestones of Growth

A journey driven by commitment, innovation, and trust.

2006

Company Founded

Established regional transport operations in Aluva, Kerala, starting with a customer-first commitment and a small dedicated fleet.

2010

Fleet Expansion

Expanded cargo capabilities and carrier asset base to serve critical high-capacity freight corridors across South India.

2015

Corporate Client Acquisition

Secured key long-term contracts with major enterprises, establishing VKT as a trusted provider of corporate employee mobility.

2020

Technology Integration

Integrated advanced telemetry systems, real-time GPS fleet tracking, and digitized route optimization to elevate supply chain transparency.

2025

Predator Holidays Launch

Launched our premium passenger travel division, introducing luxury touring coaches and curated leisure travel packages.

2026

VKT Logistics LLP Formation

Restructured as a Limited Liability Partnership to power our next phase of national scale, end-to-end logistics solutions, and tech-driven platforms.
