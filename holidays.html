Signature Journeys

Popular Holiday Circuits & Itineraries

Curated travel corridors designed for corporate retreats, family escapes, and group exploration.

3 DAYS / 2 NIGHTS

Munnar-Thekkady Hills Circuit

Traverse deep tea valleys, spice gardens, wildlife reserves, and scenic mountain roads. Includes premium resort stays and guided plantation treks.

Daily Itinerary Schedule
D1

Kochi to Munnar: Tea estate check-in, Valara & Cheeyappara waterfalls, evening campfire.

D2

Munnar Exploration: Eravikulam National Park (Nilgiri Tahr), Mattupetty Dam, speed boating.

D3

Thekkady & Return: Periyar Tiger Reserve boat safari, spice garden tour, return to Kochi.

Vehicle Fleet: Luxury Bus / Traveler
4 DAYS / 3 NIGHTS

Wayanad Adventure & Safari

Explore historical caves, waterfalls, bamboo forests, and wildlife reserves. Tailor-made for college tour groups and corporate team outings.

Daily Itinerary Schedule
D1

Arrival & Heights: Drive to Wayanad, Lakkidi Viewpoint, chain tree, jungle resort check-in.

D2

Caves & Water: Banasura Sagar Dam (earthen), Edakkal Caves ancient carvings, bamboo rafting.

D3

Wild Safari: Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary morning safari, Kuruva Island stream walks.

D4

Falls & Depart: Pookode Lake boating, Soochipara Waterfalls hike, Kalpetta shopping, departure.

Vehicle Fleet: Semi-Sleeper / Volvo
5 DAYS / 4 NIGHTS

South India Pilgrimage Corridor

A highly coordinated, comfortable temple circuit covering Madurai, Rameshwaram, and Kanyakumari. High-density safety checks and expert guides.

Daily Itinerary Schedule
D1

Kochi to Madurai: Drive to Madurai heritage hub, evening Meenakshi Temple darshan.

D2

Madurai to Rameshwaram: Ramanathaswamy Temple ritual bath, sunset at Dhanushkodi beach.

D3

Rameshwaram to Kanyakumari: Drive to lands-end beach, Triveni Sangam, sunset viewpoint.

D4

Memorials & Town: Vivekananda Rock Memorial ferry, Thiruvalluvar Statue, local markets.

D5

Suchindram & Return: Sthanumalaya Temple visit, return drive to Aluva/Kochi.

Vehicle Fleet: Volvo AC Sleeper Fleet
2 DAYS / 1 NIGHT

Alleppey Backwaters Cruise

Relax on traditional Kettuvallam houseboats gliding through palm-fringed canals. Indulge in authentic local cuisine and serene lagoon views.

Daily Itinerary Schedule
D1

Kochi to Alleppey: Board luxury wooden houseboat at noon, backwater cruise, traditional Kerala lunch, sunset on lagoon.

D2

Kumarakom & Return: Morning cruise, bird sanctuary nature walk, toddy shop culinary experience, return transfer to Kochi.

Vehicle Fleet: Executive Traveler / Sedan
4 DAYS / 3 NIGHTS

Ooty-Coonoor Hill Station Retreat

Escape to the cool Nilgiri hills. Enjoy the iconic mountain toy train, sprawling tea estates, colonial architecture, and refreshing waterfalls.

Daily Itinerary Schedule
D1

Kochi to Ooty: Scenic drive via Nilgiris, Pykara Lake boating, resort check-in, botanical gardens walk.

D2

Peaks & Lakes: Doddabetta Peak panoramic view, Ooty Lake boating, chocolate factory & tea museum tour.

D3

Toy Train Ride: Nilgiri Mountain Railway (Toy Train) ride to Coonoor, Dolphin's Nose viewpoint, Sims Park.

D4

Tea Estates & Return: Highfield tea factory walk, spice shopping at Commercial Road, drive back to Kochi.

Vehicle Fleet: Luxury Coach / Traveler

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common operational questions regarding our holiday rentals.

You can easily request a rental through our Contact Form on the contact page, by sending an email to our dedicated booking office at tours@vktlogistics.com, or by calling us at +91 73566 97973. We will assess your route, itinerary details, passenger size (e.g., 12, 17, 26, 36, or 49 seats), and return a custom quote within 24 hours.
All our Predator Holidays vehicles undergo daily mechanical audits before departure. Our luxury coaches are equipped with certified speed governors, functional emergency exits, first-aid boxes, and GPS. Additionally, we only employ verified, experienced heavy-vehicle drivers who pass routine health and driving reviews.
Rescheduling or cancellation of passenger vehicles is free of charge up to 72 hours prior to the scheduled departure time. Cancellations made between 24 and 72 hours incur a 25% charge of the booking amount, while cancellations under 24 hours are subject to a 50% retention. Special peak-season booking policies may vary.
